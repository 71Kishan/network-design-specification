# Implementation Timeline

The original project included a proposed implementation plan for the physical-design stage. This timeline represents **estimated work**, not work completed by the student team.

| Workstream | Estimated Hours | Estimated Duration |
|---|---:|---:|
| Purchase equipment | 4 | 4 days |
| Server-room management | 18 | 3 days |
| Cabling | 12 | 2 days |
| Wireless | 3 | 1 day |
| Workstation management | 16 | 2 days |
| Ergonomics | 11 | 2 days |
| **Documented total** | **64** | **14 days** |

## Planned Work Sequence

### 1. Equipment procurement

Acquire the selected connectivity, workstation, server-rack, and ergonomic equipment.

### 2. Server-room management

Plan and organize the server-room infrastructure around the proposed rack and retained network equipment.

### 3. Cabling

Apply the physical design to the existing Cat5e and fiber infrastructure and complete required cabling work.

### 4. Wireless

Address the wireless design and access-point placement issues identified during the Wi-Fi mapping exercise.

### 5. Workstation management

Replace the planned workstation fleet and manage the transition from the existing leased Dell Inspiron 5000 devices.

### 6. Ergonomics

Deploy the proposed adjustable-height desks as part of the workstation-area improvement plan.

## Important Status Boundary

The project documentation describes these activities as part of the proposed implementation phase. The student team completed the design/planning work but did **not** perform the physical installation, configuration, testing, or maintenance. Therefore, the timeline must not be presented as a completed deployment schedule.
